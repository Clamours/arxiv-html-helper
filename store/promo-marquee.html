1  Introduction

Large language models have shown remarkable ability to follow instructions, yet their reasoning remains brittle when the task requires many intermediate steps.

大语言模型在遵循指令方面表现出色，但当任务需要许多中间步骤时，其推理能力仍然脆弱。

In this work, we propose a simple training recipe that teaches models to verify each step before moving on.

核心动机：多步推理容易出错，对比第 4 节的误差累积实验。
